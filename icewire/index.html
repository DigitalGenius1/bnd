THE CATEGORY IS CONVERGING

Icewire does not require pretending nobody else exists.

Controllers, DCIM, historians, digital twins and AI assistants already solve important pieces. That is evidence that the stack is ready to converge.

THE POSITION: the vendor-neutral intelligence layer between local machine control and broad facility management—built around the behaviour of liquid.

The opportunity is a transition, not a headline forecast.

Measure the market bottom-up: liquid-cooled sites entering operation × loops per site × integration and software value × fleet expansion. The first job is to prove the wedge, not decorate a deck with an unearned TAM.
